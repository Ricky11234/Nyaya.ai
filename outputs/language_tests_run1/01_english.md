- Contact the seller’s customer‑service (by phone or email) and remind them of the pending refund, attaching any proof of return (tracking number, receipt, photos of the damaged mixer).  
- If the seller does not respond within a reasonable time (e.g., 7‑10 days), send a **formal written notice** (email or registered post) demanding the refund and stating that you may file a complaint under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.  
- You can lodge a complaint online at the **National Consumer Helpline (NCH)** (https://consumerhelpline.gov.in) or at the **Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum** (district, state or national level, depending on the amount). The filing fee is nominal (around ₹100‑₹500).  
- Provide the forum with copies of the order invoice, proof of return shipment, tracking details, and any communication with the seller. The forum can order the seller to refund the amount, pay interest, and may also award compensation for inconvenience.  
- While the dispute is pending, you may also ask your bank to **block** the transaction amount (if it was a card payment) under the “charge‑back” or “dispute” process, citing non‑delivery of service.  
- Keep all records (emails, screenshots, courier receipts) as they will be needed for any legal or consumer‑forum proceeding.
