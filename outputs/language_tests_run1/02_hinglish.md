**What you can do if a seller in India hasn’t refunded you after two months and isn’t responding:**

- **Keep a record** – Save every email, chat, order confirmation, and note the dates you tried to contact the seller.  
- **Use the platform’s help** – If you bought the item on a marketplace (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, eBay), raise a formal refund/“item not received” request through their customer‑service portal. Most platforms will intervene and issue a refund if the seller does not reply within a few days.  
- **Contact your payment provider** – If you paid by credit card, debit card, net banking, or a wallet (e.g., Paytm, PhonePe), open a dispute with the bank or payment gateway. They can reverse the transaction under “non‑delivery of goods.”  
- **File a complaint under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019** – You can approach the nearest District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum (DCDRF) with your documents. The forum can order the seller to refund you and may award a small compensation for inconvenience.  
- **Report to the cyber‑crime cell** – If the seller is deliberately avoiding you and the amount is significant, you may lodge an FIR with the local police or the cyber‑crime cell, citing the Consumer Protection Act and the Information Technology Act.
