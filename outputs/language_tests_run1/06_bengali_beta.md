- **Ask for the reason in writing** – Your brother has the right to know why he is being detained (Section 41 of the CrPC). Ask the officer to give the grounds and ask for a copy of the arrest memo.  
- **Call a lawyer immediately** – A criminal defence lawyer can file a petition for bail or for his release and can check whether the police had valid “probable cause.”  
- **Get the arrest details** – Write down the date, time, place, officer’s name, badge/ID number and police station. This will help the lawyer and any later complaint.  
- **File a written complaint** – If you believe the arrest was unlawful, lodge a complaint with the Superintendent of Police or the State Human Rights Commission.  
- **Seek bail** – The lawyer can apply for bail under Section 439 of the CrPC (or Section 167 for police custody) to get your brother out of custody while the case is examined.  
- **Consider a legal challenge** – If the arrest was without any legal basis, the lawyer may move the court to quash the FIR and may also file a civil suit for wrongful detention under the Constitution (Article 21) and the Indian Penal Code.
