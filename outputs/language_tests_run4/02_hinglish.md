## Nyaya.ai
- Apne order ka invoice, payment proof aur seller ke saath ki gayi chat/email screenshots save kar lo.  
- Seller ko ek final reminder bhejo (email ya message) ki refund 7 din ke andar nahi mila toh aap consumer complaint file karenge.  
- Refund na milne ki complaint online e‑Jagriti (e‑jagriti.gov.in) par file karo – yeh consumer dispute ke liye free portal hai.  
- Complaint file karte waqt seller ka naam, order details, aur aapka refund amount clearly mention karo.  
- District Commission aapki complaint ko admit karega, fir seller ko apna side dene ke liye 30 din (ya extendable 15 din) ka time dega.  
- Agar seller respond nahi karta ya dispute settle nahi hota, to Commission aapke favour mein order pass karega aur seller ko refund dene ko kahega.
