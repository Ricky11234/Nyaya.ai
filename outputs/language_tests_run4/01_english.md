## Nyaya.ai
- Keep the order receipt, the broken‑product photos, the return‑shipping proof and any emails or messages with the seller.  
- Try contacting the seller again (phone or email) and ask for the refund in writing, reminding them of their duty to return your money for a defective product.  
- If the seller does not reply or refuses, file an online consumer complaint on **e‑Jagriti** (e‑jagriti.gov.in). Attach all the documents you kept.  
- The consumer commission will first try mediation; if that fails, it will hear the case and can order the seller to pay the refund and any compensation.  
- You can also call the National Consumer Helpline at **1915** for free advice and help with the filing process.  

**Based on: Consumer Protection Act, 2019**
