**BNSS (Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita) ke tahat anticipatory bail ke conditions – detailed points**

- **Section 482 BNSS ka provision** – Ye section, jo pehle Section 438 CrPC ke barabar tha, “anticipatory bail” ka right grant karta hai, jisme applicant ko arrest ke pehle hi bail mil sakti hai. (Source: Adv. Dhanashree – “Anticipatory Bail Under BNSS Section 482”)

- **Eligibility – non‑bailable offence** – Applicant ko tabhi anticipatory bail mil sakti hai jab wo “non‑bailable offence” ke liye arrest ka dar ho. (Source: Wikipedia – Anticipatory bail)

- **Application ka forum** – Application High Court ya Court of Session mein file ki jati hai, jahan court ko “direction” dene ka power hota hai ki arrest hone par applicant ko bail milni chahiye. (Source: Adv. Dhanashree)

- **Opposition ko notice** – Anticipatory bail ki filing ke baad opposing party (usually the complainant or the public prosecutor) ko notice milta hai, aur wo bail application ko contest kar sakti hai. (Source: Wikipedia)

- **Court‑imposed conditions – general**  
  - Applicant ko investigation ke saath full cooperation karni hogi.  
  - Kisi bhi witness, evidence ya police officer ko influence ya tamper nahi karna.  
  - Passport ya koi bhi travel document surrender karna, ya court ki permission ke bina travel na karna.  
  - Court‑specified monetary surety ya property bond dena, jab zaroori ho.  
  (Source: Supreme Court judgment *Radha Krishan Dwivedi v. State of Madhya Pradesh* (8 Oct 2026) – “subject to conditions, including cooperation with the investigation.”; also IJRIAS paper on bail under BNSS)

- **Specific condition – no tampering with evidence** – Courts frequently condition bail on “no tampering with evidence or influencing witnesses,” jo ki BNSS ke jurisprudence mein standard hai. (Source: IJRIAS – “Objectives and Scope of Bail Under BNSS, 2023”)

- **Time‑bound condition** – Bail direction tab tak valid hoti hai jab tak arrest nahi hota; arrest hone par bail automatically grant ho jati hai, jab tak court ne koi revocation order nahi diya. (Source: Adv. Dhanashree)

- **Revocation power** – Agar applicant condition violate karta hai (jaise investigation ko obstruct karna), to court bail ko revoke kar sakti hai. (Source: IJRIAS)

- **No automatic entitlement** – Supreme Court ne baar‑baar emphasize kiya hai ki anticipatory bail “discretionary” hai; har case ki facts aur seriousness of offence ko dekh kar hi bail grant hoti hai. (Source: IJRIAS & Radha Krishan Dwivedi judgment)

- **Special law considerations** – Agar offence koi special law (jaise NIA, NDPS) ke under aata hai, to court ko us special law ki bail provisions ko bhi dhyan mein rakhna hota hai, lekin BNSS ke Section 482 ka basic framework phir bhi apply hota hai. (Source: PDF “Bail – Comparative Study of CrPC, 1973 and BNSS, 2023”)

- **Procedural safeguard** – Applicant ko bail application ke saath affidavit ya declaration dena hota hai ki usne sabhi facts sachche tarike se bataye hain, aur koi false statement nahi di. (Source: IndianKanoon – Section 482 document)

**Summary:** BNSS ke Section 482 ke tahat anticipatory bail milne ke liye applicant ko non‑bailable offence ka dar hona chahiye, application High Court/Session Court mein file karni hoti hai, opposition ko notice milta hai, aur court ko discretion hoti hai ki wo bail grant kare ya na kare. Grant hone par court specific conditions impose kar sakti hai – investigation ke saath cooperation, no witness tampering, travel restrictions, surety, etc. In conditions ka violation bail revocation ka ground ban sakta hai.
