## Nyaya.ai
- File a complaint online at **e‑Jagriti** (the portal for consumer disputes).  
- Attach the order receipt, proof that the mixer was broken, and any emails or messages about the return and refund.  
- The consumer board will first try to settle the matter by mediation; if the seller agrees, a written settlement will be signed.  
- If mediation fails, the case will go to the District Commission, which will hear the dispute and can order the seller to pay the refund.  
- Keep copies of everything and, if needed, ask for free legal help from NALSA (call 15100).  

**Based on: Consumer Protection Act, 2019**
