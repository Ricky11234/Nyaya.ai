**BNSS ke tahat anticipatory bail grant karne ki conditions (Section 482 BNSS, Section 480 BNSS)**  

- **Reasonable apprehension of arrest** – Applicant ko genuine aur reasonable darr hona chahiye ki wo kisi non‑bailable offence ke liye arrest ho sakta hai. *(Kapildixitco.com)*  
- **Pre‑arrest filing** – Application ko arrest hone se pehle hi file karna zaroori hai; arrest ke baad anticipatory bail ka prayog nahi hota. *(Kapildixitco.com)*  
- **No habitual offender status** – Court generally aise vyakti ko bail nahi deti jiska criminal record bahut zyada ho ya jo repeat offender ho. *(Kapildixitco.com)*  
- **Restriction on foreign travel** – Court applicant ko bina specific permission ke desh se bahar travel karne se rok sakti hai. *(Kapildixitco.com)*  
- **Surrender of passport & reporting** – Passport surrender karna, police station ya designated authority ko regular reporting karna, aur jurisdiction ke bahar na jaana aam conditions hain. *(LawBhoomi.com)*  
- **Surety/Bond requirement** – Applicant ko appropriate surety ya bail bond furnish karna hota hai; amount court ke discretion par hota hai. *(LawBhoomi.com)*  
- **Section 480 BNSS ke specific conditions** – Section 480 BNSS bail ke liye detailed conditions list karta hai, jo anticipatory bail par bhi lagu hoti hain (e.g., reporting schedule, non‑tampering with evidence). *(LawBhoomi.com)*  
- **Additional conditions under Section 480(3)** – Court extra conditions impose kar sakti hai, jaise ki Director of Prosecution se consult karna in serious cases. *(LawBhoomi.com)*  
- **Section 482 BNSS (formerly Sec. 438 CrPC)** – Anticipatory bail ka statutory provision ab Section 482 BNSS mein hai; High Court ya Sessions Court iske under relief grant kar sakti hai. *(Kapildixitco.com)*  
- **Cancellation power – Section 483(3) BNSS** – Agar accused bail ke dauran koi aur offence commit karta hai ya conditions violate karta hai, to bail ko cancel kiya ja sakta hai. *(LawBhoomi.com)*  
- **Judicial oversight** – Serious cases mein court ko Director of Prosecution ko involve karna pad sakta hai, taaki bail conditions investigation ko hamper na kare. *(GoLegalConsultancy.com)*
