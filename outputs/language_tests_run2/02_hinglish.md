## Nyaya.ai
- Seller ko ek formal reminder bhejo (email ya registered post) aur refund ki demand likho, saare order details aur payment proof attach karo.  
- Agar 15‑30 din tak koi response nahi milta, to aap Consumer Forum (District Commission) me online complaint file kar sakte ho.  
- Complaint file karte waqt apne order confirmation, payment receipt, chat screenshots aur seller ke non‑response ka record attach karna na bhoolen.  
- Forum aapki complaint ko admit karega (Section 69) aur seller ko apna side dene ka 30‑45 din ka time dega; fir settlement ya hearing ho sakti hai.  
- Agar aapko settlement pasand nahi aata ya seller nahi manta, to aap appeal ya review ke liye bhi apply kar sakte ho (Section 40‑41).
