**Anticipatory Bail – Conditions under the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)**  

- **Application ke liye eligibility** – Jab koi vyakti ko lagta hai ki usko non‑bailable offence ke liye arrest kiya ja sakta hai, tab wo High Court ya Court of Session me anticipatory bail ke liye apply kar sakta hai.  [Document 2, p. 162]  

- **Court ka discretion** – Court, apni sujhav ke mutabiq, agar fit samjhe to direction de sakti hai ki arrest hone par vyakti ko bail mil jaye.  [Document 2, p. 162]  

- **Condition‑specific direction** – Bail direction me court case ki facts ke hisaab se koi bhi condition shamil kar sakti hai (jaise attendance, surety, etc.).  [Document 2, p. 162]  

- **Attendance requirement** – Bail grant hone par vyakti ko bond ke terms ke mutabiq court ya police ke samne regular attendance karni hogi.  [Document 1, p. 161]  

- **No commission of similar offence** – Bail ke dauran vyakti ko ussi ya uske jaisa koi bhi offence commit nahi karna chahiye.  [Document 1, p. 161]  

- **Bond execution & surety** – Vyakti ko ek aisa bond execute karna hoga jisme police officer ya Court ko lagta hai ki sufficient sum aur surety (agar zaroori ho) ho, taki wo apni appearance guarantee kar sake.  [Document 3, p. 163]  

- **Reasonable bond amount** – Bond ki rakam case ki circumstances ko dhyan me rakhte hue fix ki jayegi aur wo excessive nahi honi chahiye.  [Document 3, p. 163]  

- **Possibility of reduction** – High Court ya Court of Session bail ki rakam ko kam karne ka adhikar rakhti hai, agar wo zaroori samjhe.  [Document 3, p. 163]  

- **Inclusion of conditions in bond** – Jo bhi condition court impose karti hai, usko bond ke terms me likhna anivarya hai.  [Document 3, p. 163]  

- **Forfeiture on non‑appearance** – Agar bail ke baad vyakti court me appear nahi karta, to bond forfeited ho jayega aur Section 493 ke tahat procedure lagu hoga.  [Document 2, p. 162]  

- **Revocation & re‑arrest** – Court ya High Court, agar bail conditions violate hoti hain, to bail ko cancel kar ke vyakti ko dobara arrest kar sakti hai.  [Document 3, p. 163]  

- **Special mention of sections 64, 66, 70 of Bhartiya Nyaya Sanhita** – Anticipatory bail applications generally fall under these sections, aur hearing ke samay court in sections ko refer karta hai.  [Document 3, p. 163]
