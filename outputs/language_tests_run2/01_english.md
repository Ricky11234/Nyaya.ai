- **Ask the seller again**: Send a clear email or message reminding them of the refund, attach photos of the broken mixer and the return receipt, and set a short deadline (for example, 7 days).  
- **Use the platform’s grievance officer**: Most e‑commerce sites have a grievance officer. Raise the issue through the site’s help centre and ask the officer to intervene.  
- **File a complaint on the E‑Daakhil portal**: This is the online system for consumer complaints under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. You can upload all your evidence (order details, return proof, communication) and the complaint will be sent to the appropriate consumer dispute redressal commission.  
- **Seek compensation**: If the seller or platform still refuses, the consumer commission can order a refund plus a small amount for inconvenience.  
- **Consider a credit‑card or bank dispute**: If you paid by card, you can also raise a dispute with your bank, asking them to reverse the charge because the goods were not delivered in a usable condition.  

These steps follow the consumer rights framework that applies to e‑commerce transactions in India.
