**Can you win a Rs 5 lakh claim against your neighbour?**  

- A neighbour who owns a tree has a duty to keep it healthy and safe. If the tree is dead, diseased, or obviously unsafe, the owner must take steps to cut or support it.  
- If the tree falls and damages your car, you can sue the neighbour for negligence **if you can show** (a) the neighbour knew or should have known the tree was a danger, and (b) the neighbour failed to act to prevent the fall.  
- The fact that a storm caused the tree to fall does not automatically excuse the neighbour. Courts look at whether the danger was **foreseeable** and whether reasonable care could have been taken before the storm.  
- The Supreme Court has said that a falling tree on a vehicle is **not** a “motor‑vehicle accident” for claims under Section 166 of the Motor Vehicles Act. So you cannot rely on that section; you must rely on ordinary tort law (negligence).  
- If you can prove the neighbour’s negligence, you may claim the cost of repairing the car (Rs 5 lakh) plus any other losses. The claim is a civil suit, and you must file it within the limitation period (normally three years from the date of damage).  

**Next steps**  

1. Gather evidence that the tree was unhealthy or that the neighbour was warned (e.g., photos, neighbour’s notices, municipal reports).  
2. Get a professional assessment of the tree’s condition before the storm.  
3. Send a legal notice to the neighbour demanding compensation.  
4. If the neighbour does not pay, file a civil suit for damages in the appropriate civil court.
