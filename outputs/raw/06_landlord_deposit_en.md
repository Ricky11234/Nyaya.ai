## Nyaya.ai
- Send the landlord a written notice asking for the full Rs 60,000 back, reminding him that the tenancy agreement does not allow any deduction for painting when there is no damage.  
- Attach copies of the rent agreement, the move‑out photos, and any proof of payment of the deposit.  
- Tell the landlord that, under the Model Tenancy Act, the security deposit must be returned within 15 days of the notice period ending, after deducting only genuine liabilities.  
- If he still refuses, file an application with the local Rent Authority (or Rent Tribunal) for the return of the deposit and for any penalty the Act allows.  
- Keep a copy of your notice, the landlord’s reply, and all supporting documents to show the Rent Authority.

**1. Facts**

1.5. Despite my compliance, you have refused to return the security deposit, alleging that the walls require painting. No written notice specifying any damage, nor any itemised cost of painting, has been provided to me.  

**2. Legal Position**  

2.1. Under the *Model Tenancy Act, 2021* (applicable in the State of Maharashtra), the security deposit “shall be refunded to the tenant on the date of taking over vacant possession of the premises from the tenant, after making due deduction of any liability of the tenant” (Section 2(2), p. 8).  

2.2. Further, the Act provides that the landlord must refund the security deposit “within a period of fifteen days of the expiry of the notice period, after making due deduction of liability of the tenant, if any” (Section 10, p. 10).  

2.3. In the present case, no liability on my part has been established. The alleged requirement of painting is not supported by any written claim, itemised estimate, or evidence of damage beyond normal wear and tear. Consequently, I am entitled to a full refund of the entire deposit of Rs 60,000.  

**3. Demand**  

In view of the foregoing, I hereby demand that you:

- Refund the full security deposit of Rs 60,000 (Rupees Sixty Thousand only) to me, **within fifteen (15) days** from the receipt of this notice, i.e., on or before [DATE – 15 DAYS FROM NOTICE DATE].  

- Make the payment by way of a bank transfer to the following account:

**4. Consequence of Non‑Compliance**  

If the above amount is not received within the stipulated period, I shall be constrained to initiate appropriate legal proceedings before the competent Rent Court/Consumer Forum for recovery of the deposit, together with interest, costs of litigation, and any other relief that the forum may deem fit, without any further notice.
