- Apne phone ka bill, warranty card aur service centre ka written refusal ka photo/scan sambhal ke rakho.  
- Phone ko ek independent authorised service centre ya technician se check karvao aur likhit report le lo ki damage “physical” nahi balki manufacturing defect hai.

### 3. Cause of Action  

The cause of action arises under Chapter VI (Product Liability) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019:  

* **Section 82** – The complainant is entitled to file a product liability action for harm caused by a defective product.  

* **Section 83** – The action may be brought against the product service provider (the opposite party) for a defect in the product.  

* **Section 84(1)(d)** – The phone does not conform to the express warranty provided at the time of sale.  

* **Section 85** – The service provider is liable because the service rendered (refusal to honour the warranty) is “defective… and does not conform to the express warranty or the terms and conditions of the contract.”  

The opposite party’s refusal to honour the warranty, despite the defect being covered, constitutes a breach of the express warranty and a deficiency in service, giving rise to liability under the above sections.
