### 2. Facts  

1. The complainant purchased a **[PHONE BRAND AND MODEL]** mobile phone for **Rs. 18,000** during a Diwali sale on **[DATE OF PURCHASE]**.  
2. The purchase was made from an authorized dealer/retailer and a valid invoice/receipt was issued (copy attached).  
3. Two weeks after purchase, on **[DATE SCREEN BROKE]**, the phone’s screen cracked spontaneously; the phone was not dropped or subjected to any physical impact (the complainant can produce CCTV footage/witness statements to prove this).  
4. The complainant approached the authorized service centre **[SERVICE CENTER NAME]** for repair under the express warranty.  
5. The service centre refused to repair or replace the phone, stating that the damage was “physical” and therefore not covered under warranty, despite the complainant’s assertion that the damage was not due to any mishandling.  
6. The service centre issued a written communication (copy attached) denying warranty service.  

### 3. Cause of Action  

The cause of action arises under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**:  

* **Section 82** – The complaint is a product liability claim as the phone is a defective product.  
* **Section 84(d)** – The phone does **not conform to the express warranty** given at the time of sale.  
* **Section 85(a) & (d)** – The product service provider (the service centre) has provided a deficient service by refusing to honour the warranty and the service does not conform to the express warranty terms.  

The refusal to honour the warranty amounts to a deficiency in service and a breach of the express warranty, causing loss and inconvenience to the consumer.

### 4. Jurisdiction  

The value of the goods involved (Rs. 18,000) is within the pecuniary jurisdiction of the **District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission** as per the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Hence, this Commission is the appropriate forum for the present complaint.

### 5. Relief Sought  

The complainant respectfully prays that the Commission may be pleased to:  

1. **Direct the opposite party** to repair the phone **under the express warranty** at no cost to the complainant, or alternatively, **replace the phone** with a brand‑new unit of the same model and specifications.  
2. **Award compensation** of **Rs. 18,000** (the purchase price) for the loss suffered due to the refusal of warranty service, if repair/replacement is not feasible.  
3. **Award costs of the proceedings** and any other relief that the Commission deems just and proper.

2. **Photographs of the damaged screen**.

4. **Evidence that the phone was not dropped** – CCTV footage / witness statements (if available).
