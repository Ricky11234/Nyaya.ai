**What the notice means and what you should do**

- The bank says you owe Rs 2.3 lakh on a credit‑card you say you never applied for.  
- Under RBI rules, a bank cannot issue a credit‑card without your explicit consent. If you never gave consent, the card should be closed at no cost to you.  
- Write a short letter to the bank (by registered post or email) stating that you never applied for the card, you have not activated it, and you deny any liability. Ask the bank to close the account and confirm in writing that the amount is cancelled.  
- Keep a copy of your letter, the notice, and any reply. If the bank does not close the account within **7 working days** of your request, you can treat the account as fraudulent.  
- If the bank does not resolve the matter within **30 days** of your complaint, you may file a complaint with the RBI Banking Ombudsman (online or by post).  
- You can also lodge a consumer complaint on the e‑Jagriti portal (e‑jagriti.gov.in). Since the amount is below Rs 50 lakh, the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission has jurisdiction.  
- If you need free legal advice, call NALSA at **15100**.  

**Quick checklist**

1. Send a denial letter to the bank, ask for closure and written confirmation.  
2. Keep all documents and send the letter by a traceable method.  
3. If no reply or the bank refuses, file a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman (within 30 days).  
4. If still unresolved, file a consumer complaint on e‑Jagriti (District Commission).
